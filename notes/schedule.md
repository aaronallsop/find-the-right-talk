## How to tell if you are ahead or behind

Every 15 minutes, look at the **Done** list on the board and compare it with the
**milestones** below. The question is only: *is the piece that should be done by now, done?*
Done means it worked on screen and was saved (by Neptune before 12:00, Titan after), not
"nearly there".

**What to do with the answer:**

- **On time or up to 15 minutes behind:** carry on. That is normal.
- **30 minutes behind:** cut the next "if time" piece now. First to go is M5 (the flip), then
  M4 (the start-ups toggle). Tell Saturn which one, and Rhea moves it to "Blocked: cut".
- **45 minutes or more behind:** stop and talk to Saturn. Skip the 14:00 panel. The page (M3)
  becomes the stretch, and the demo runs from Terminal.
- **Ahead:** do not add anything. Use the time in this order: submit early (if the form allows
  edits), rehearse more, then M5. The fit check (S1) only if you said yes to it tonight.
- **Never move 15:45 or 16:30.** Those two times hold whatever the state.
- **If Saturn goes quiet** while Titan or Cassini are still working, send Saturn any short
  message ("carry on"). The app holds the others' reports after a run of them with nothing
  from you, and your message releases them (sessions note, "two habits of the app").
