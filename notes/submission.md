## The paragraph

**Find the Right Talk, for the press.** A media outlet's profile says what it covers. This tool
reads the KubeCon programme (601 sessions, 715 speakers, 226 sponsors) and [ranks the talks,
speakers and companies that fit an outlet, each with the passage that made it match]. Start-up
sponsors are marked, so a small company with the right story can surface beside the big names.
The programme can be searched three ways: by words, by meaning, and both blended. [A page with
two doors, "I'm press" and "I'm a vendor": a picker and two boxes to type in; nothing typed is
stored.] It runs on Elastic Cloud serverless; [a copy on a laptop is the fallback if the wifi
fails]. [Elastic's own models: only if built.]
